Draft challenge rules

Fat-ass style, with a taco twist

These intentionally simple rules mirror the spirit of established Taco 50K events. The final version will be published before registration opens.

  1. 01

    Participants must be at least 13 on event day. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian’s consent.

  2. 02

    Check in before your assigned start. Check-in opens at 6:30 AM and the first wave leaves at 7:00 AM.

  3. 03

    Visit every checkpoint in the published order. Your completed route must total at least 31.1 miles.

  4. 04

    Eat one complete Taco Bell menu item at every food stop. Finish it before leaving that location.

  5. 05

    Required milestone items still count. The final route guide will identify the larger required items at designated stops.

  6. 06

    Customizing food is allowed. Drinks, sauces, and individual add-ons do not count as the required menu item.

  7. 07

    Mobile preordering is encouraged. Use the Taco Bell app or order on site without blocking normal restaurant operations.

  8. 08

    Document each stop. Upload a clear wrapper, receipt, or checkpoint photo in the Runner Hub.

  9. 09

    Finish within 11 hours. Stop your watch only after completing the final required item.

  10. 10

    This is an open-course, self-supported challenge. Carry what you need and arrange your own transportation if you withdraw.

  11. 11

    Obey all pedestrian and traffic laws. Use sidewalks and crossings, follow volunteer directions, and never enter a closed or unsafe section.

  12. 12

    Be kind and leave no trace. Respect restaurant teams, other guests, volunteers, neighborhoods, and the course.

Challenge awards are for fun, not speed. Participants may stop an add-on challenge at any time without affecting their regular GLTaco50K finish. Never delay prescribed or medically necessary treatment to complete a challenge; taking needed medication simply ends the Bubble Gut attempt.

Open-course safety

No closed roads. No guaranteed right of way.

Participants share public sidewalks, crossings, trails, and restaurant spaces with everyone else. Every runner and walker is responsible for traffic awareness, weather preparation, hydration, nutrition, and knowing when to stop.

Routes, crossings, emergency plans, event capacity, and municipality requirements remain under review. Published maps are planning previews—not final turn-by-turn directions.

Good questions

Before you lace up

Is registration open?

Not yet. Registration will open after both routes are field-checked and event requirements are confirmed with the appropriate agencies and property owners.

How many people can participate?

The final capacity has not been set. Participants will be released in groups of no more than 25 to reduce sidewalk, crossing, and restaurant congestion.

Can I walk it?

Yes. Runners and walkers are welcome, but everyone must complete the route and required food within the final published cutoff.

Do I buy my own food?

Yes. This is a self-supported challenge; participants purchase their own required food and carry their own water and supplies.

Will roads be closed?

The current concept uses open public routes without full road closures. Final course design and any traffic controls will depend on agency review and field conditions.

What does the Runner Hub do?

It is the event companion app for registration, route details, checkpoint proof, progress tracking, safety information, and event updates. Access will be fully opened when registration launches.
